Your Purpose

MSC Cruises is seeking for an Intern to join the Finance Transformation Department. His/her main duty is to support the Program Management team in their daily activities mainly related to the global implementation of S/4 HANA (O2C, P2P, R2RC and C2RTreasury) and the potential involvement in other parallel initiatives..

Your Impact

  • S/4 HANA implementation & other related business initiatives.
  • Support the Project Management central team in its daily duties across different project phases (solution build, test, training, go-live…) with particular focus on: Test monitoring, extracting status from JIRA, target operating model (TOM) design and maintenance, pre and post Go-live assistance.
  • Build and maintain project documentation (project plan, dashboards, procedures, communication plan, newsletters, slides etc.) and keep the shared library updated.
  • Participate and help to prepare periodic alignments and governance meetings.
  • Work closely with other finance business team members in order to track decisions taken, risks and actions.
  • Collaborate to maintain the issue log and to perform related follow-up.
  • Assist the team in the organisational and logistic tasks (meeting scheduling, room booking, attendees tracking, minutes preparation).
  • Finance Portal SharePoint : Support finance department members by collecting feedback, proposals, changing requests and ensuring that all documents are up to date.
  • Cooperate with the team in the portal On-Going maintenance.
  • Team management & Control-Tower (pipeline of projects / initiatives managed by the Finance Transformation team)
  • Collaborate in the weekly meeting organisation by collecting agenda and materials.
  • Track and update the initiatives and projects ( new activities, achievement…)
  • Participate to the internal and external team meetings.
  • Maintain up to date the Finance Transformation control tower ( planning, realisation, workload, deliverables etc.)
  • Department budget controlling : Collect purchase requests and create purchase orders in the MSC ERP system ( Navision )
  • Track the costs/budget related to the Finance Transformation Department.
  • Support the Finance Transformation management, the controlling, and the account payable teams by performing requested analysis.

Your Journey so far

  • Bachelor or Master Student in management, economics, business administration or in a related field.
  • Project management knowledge and/or previous experiences are a plus
  • Previous experience with ERP software is a plus (Navision, SAP etc.)
  • Fluent in English ( spoken and written )

Your Essentials

  • IT abilities and strong knowledge of Outlook, MSTeams, Sharepoint and Microsoft Office tools (Excel and PowerPoint)
  • Soft skills (proactivity, problem solving, communication, time management/organisation, motivation, curiosity)
  • EU passport or work permit for Switzerland.
