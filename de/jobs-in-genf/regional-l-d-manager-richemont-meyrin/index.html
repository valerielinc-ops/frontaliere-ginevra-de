Vollständige Beschreibung

Reference code: JR135926 The story of Cartier is founded on curiosity and passion. For more than 170 years we have embraced a bold, pioneering spirit that continues to inspire our teams across all Métiers from our boutiques to our workshops and corporate offices. Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striving to continuously enrich our Maison’s heritage by pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation.

How will you make an impact?

As Regional Learning & Development Manager, you will act as the Learning & Development Business Partner for our European markets. You will identify and prioritize local capability-building needs, connecting them with the Maison & Regional learning priorities, resources, and planning.

You will draft the L&D roadmap for each market you have in your scope, both for Retail & Office population, supporting business priorities while remaining relevant to each market.

Specifically for Office population, you will develop actionable Individual Development Plans in partnership with the local HR team.

You will additionally specifically lead the development, deployment, and continuous improvement of regional learning programs within the scope of Business and High-End skills.

You will contribute to a consistent European L&D operating model by strengthening collaboration between local HR teams, Local L&D Retail Coaches & Trainers, Regional Training Managers, and Regional L&D planning Manager, in order to create meaningful development opportunities across the region for our people.

Key Responsabilities

  1. Develop, Deploy and Maintain Regional Programs

Design, deploy, and continuously improve regional learning programs with the speciality of Business and High-End Skills. · Lead or contribute to Business Skills Learning Programs aligned with Regional & Maison priorities, including Retail Excellence practices (Clienteling, Storytelling...), Retail Talent Development programs, and any other evolving Business priorities given by the Maison & the Regional Exco. · Partner with the Regional High End (HE) Team to drive the development and deployment of the Regional High-End Academy and to harmonize Local HE Academies practices, while preserving market relevance · Maintain programs over time with a continuous improvement mindset. · Facilitate regional programs when relevant and necessary · Find, assess, brief, and lead external partners when relevant and necessary. · Organize and logistically manage the deployment of the regional programs you are responsible for · Communicate within the framework of the Regional L&D agenda to market the programs portfolio.

  1. Act as L&D Business Partner for dedicated Markets

Maintaining a comprehensive 360 understanding of the Maison and the European L&D portfolio (Business Skills, High End, but also Leadership & Management, Maison culture, Soft skills...), and supporting local HR Teams for the Markets in scope. · Translate business and people priorities into clear learning and development plans. · Draft the L&D roadmap of the year for each market in scope, both for Retail & Office population, in partnership with local HR Teams (being in lead for Office population, and in coordination for Retail population while partnering with the local L&D Retail Trainer & Coach). · Act as a connector between regional expertise, Maison programs, and local development needs. · Build a close understanding of each market's specificities, business and HR priorities, populations, and operational constraints. · In partnership with the local HR Team, and considering the local L&D Budget, guide the selection, sequencing, and deployment of the most critical capability needs. · Partner with the Regional L&D Planning Manager providing a clear visibility of market needs to support allocation of Regional Training Manager capacity, regional programs, dates, and formats… contributing to an integrated planning process that balances local demand, strategic priorities, and available resources.

  1. Lead Individual Development Plans (IDPs) for Office Staff

Leverage the Talent Review feedback with the local Head of HR to support the definition and annual planning of Individual Development Plans for office employees · Conduct one-to-one discussions with Managers to identify individual development needs and objectives. · Gather needs and translate them into actionable IDPs. · Consolidate office development needs at market level to identify individual and collective priorities as part of the Market L&D Roadmap. · Ensure IDPs are practical, appropriately documented, and connected to available local, regional, or Maison solutions.

  1. Deploy Local Training when required

Identify, design, and deploy local learning solutions when existing Maison or Regional programs do not fully address a market need, specifically for Office population. · Determine the appropriate delivery model, including internal facilitation, regional support, or external providers. · Ensure local solutions align with Maison standards and do not unnecessarily duplicate existing programs. · Manage the administration, logistics, communication, and tracking associated with learning initiatives personally launched or managed. · Prepare briefs and coordinate external learning partners. · Prepare or adapt training materials and learning decks. · Coordinate participant invitations, communications, and training logistics. · Ensure that every learning initiative is registered in LMS · Leverage a Blended Learning approach with our LXP · Monitor, registrations, participation, completion and learner’s engagement · Review data reports and participant feedback and follow up improvements. · Maintain accurate information and provide timely updates to local HR and regional L&D stakeholders.

What we are looking for

  • At least 5 years of experience in HR and L&D
  • Experience with Individual & Collective Development plans expected.
  • Experience in developing learning programs mandatory; in-class and remote facilitation skills required
  • Experience with strategic and business orientation expected.
  • Additional experience with a direct scope in Retail Training & High End a plus.
  • Languages: English native level mandatory, proficient in French expected.
  • Ability to travel 30%
