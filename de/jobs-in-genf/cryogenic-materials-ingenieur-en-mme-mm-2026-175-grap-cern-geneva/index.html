Vollständige Beschreibung

As an Experienced Project Graduate, you will contribute to materials characterisation and qualification activities supporting a wide range of CERN projects and technologies. Your responsibilities will include supporting, planning and executing studies on materials and components, covering materials procurement, fabrication and assembly qualification, as well as overseeing quality control activities for the construction of particle accelerators. Working within the Materials & Metrology team, you will investigate the microstructure, chemical composition, properties and failure mechanisms of metallic and other engineering materials using the section's advanced characterisation capabilities, including light microscopy, Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM), microanalysis, X-ray diffraction (XRD), hardness testing and instrumented indentation. You will also perform mechanical testing at both ambient and cryogenic temperatures, including fracture toughness assessments, and conduct failure analyses when required.

Your responsibilities

  • Investigate the behaviour, properties and failure mechanisms of engineering materials and components.
  • Characterise materials using advanced analytical techniques, including light and electron microscopy (SEM), Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDS), Electron Backscatter Diffraction (EBSD), Focused Ion Beam (FIB), X-ray diffraction (XRD), automated image analysis and instrumented indentation, including nanoindentation and atomic force microscopy (AFM).
  • Plan and perform mechanical testing at room and cryogenic temperatures, and help in the development of dedicated test facilities, experimental procedures, and data analysis protocols.
  • Lead the execution of failure analyses of materials and components, combining microstructural and mechanical characterisation with non-destructive examination methods, including visual inspection (VT), penetrant testing (PT), ultrasonic testing (UT), radiographic testing (RT) and computed tomography (CT).
  • Develop testing strategies and communicate results by defining appropriate test programmes, improving characterisation methodologies and presenting findings to CERN project teams, industrial partners and research institutes.

Your profile

  • Experience in materials engineering, with a strong background in performing experimental materials characterisation and mechanical testing at cryogenic temperatures.
  • Experience working in a multidisciplinary materials laboratory equipped for metallurgical analysis and mechanical testing.

Would be considered as an asset

  • Experience in advanced mechanical testing in liquid helium (fracture toughness, fatigue), including experimental implementation and evaluation of test results.
  • Experience in interpretation of NDT results (RT, PT, CT, UT) applied to assess material soundness.

Skills

  • Hands-on experience in mechanical testing of structural materials in liquid helium.
  • Ability to integrate complementary materials characterisation methods (SEM/EDS, XRD, EBSD) to address complex technical materials challenges.
  • Ability to process and interpret experimental data using scientific software and scripting tools.
  • Strong organisational skills to coordinate experimental campaigns.
  • Clear technical communication and reporting skills.
  • Analytical thinking and effective problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • You have a professional background in Materials / Mechanical Engineering (or a related field) and have either:
  • a Master's degree with 2 to 6 years of post-graduation professional experience;
  • or a PhD with no more than 3 years of post-graduation professional experience.
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.
