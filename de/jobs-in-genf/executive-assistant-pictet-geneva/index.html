Vollständige Beschreibung

Your team

Pictet Asset Management manages the assets of some of the world’s largest institutions, financial intermediaries and their clients. Our culture welcomes independent thinkers and centers around investment excellence, a long-term perspective and a dedication to client service.

Pictet Asset Management is seeking an Executive Assistant to provide primary support to the Head of Marketing & Products, with occasional coordination support for his direct reports in Geneva and London.

You will join a dynamic, international team working on exciting projects at the heart of the firm’s sales and investment activities.

You will provide proactive, detail-focused administrative and organisational support to the Head and the wider Marketing & Products team, enabling them to work effectively and ensuring seamless coordination with senior stakeholders and external contacts.

Your role

  • Manage the Head’s complex diary across time zones, proactively identifying conflicts and inefficiencies, and coordinate his travel arrangements and expenses.
  • Prepare, format, and proofread documents and presentations: apply corporate design guidelines and help ensure executive presentations are clear, polished and impactful.
  • Coordinate online and in-person meetings, including global team meetings, and manage related logistics and follow-up.
  • Support internal and selected client events, coordinating venues, invitations, suppliers and on-the-day logistics.
  • Monitor budgets, liaise with Finance and help ensure invoices are processed accurately and on time.
  • Identify repetitive administrative tasks and use AI tools to streamline the assistant’s work and the Head’s workflows.
  • Provide occasional organisational support to Head’s direct reports and undertake other administrative and organisational tasks as needed.

Your profile

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent qualification.
  • At least five years’ experience in a similar executive or team assistant role and possibly a strong interest in marketing and sales activities
  • Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage competing priorities.
  • Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly PowerPoint, and confidence using digital collaboration tools.
  • A strong eye for detail and visual presentation, with the ability to apply corporate design guidelines.
  • Practical experience with AI tools and an interest in automating repetitive tasks and improving ways of working.
  • Fluent spoken and written English, with a very good level of spoken and written French.
  • Resident in Switzerland or willing to relocate to Switzerland.

The successful candidate will be a true team player: discreet, flexible, proactive with strong relationship-building skills who will anticipate needs and remain calm and solutions-focused in a dynamic environment.
