Vollständige Beschreibung

Join Proton and build a better internet where privacy is the default

Founded in 2014 by scientists who met at CERN, Proton was built on the belief that privacy is a fundamental human right. What started as an encrypted email service has grown into an ecosystem of privacy-first products, including Proton Mail, VPN, Drive, Pass, and Calendar, used by millions of people worldwide.

We're profitable, independent, and building for the long term. Free from venture capital control and advertising-driven business models, we focus on what matters most: our users. Our work helps protect digital freedom, from everyday privacy to supporting journalists and activists in challenging environments.

Today, we're an international team of 700+ people, with offices across 9 countries. We have high standards for the people we hire and the work we do. You'll work alongside talented, driven colleagues who challenge each other, take ownership, and care about getting things right.

Explore our open-source projects.

Your impact:

We’re hiring Mobile Product Engineers at senior and staff levels. If you’ve led complex technical projects and want to keep building hands-on, we’d love to hear from you. Your scope and level will reflect your experience and the impact you can bring.

As a Senior Mobile Product Engineer, you’ll help shape what we build and own work from understanding the problem through to release and improvement. You’ll speak directly with customers, collaborate with Product and Design, and work across the parts of the stack needed to deliver a complete mobile experience.

What you will do:

  • Understand and shape the problem: Work with Product, Design and customers to understand user needs, question assumptions and decide what is worth building.
  • Own complete mobile features: Turn problems into practical solutions in our iOS and Android apps and the services and data behind them. Take work through testing, App Store and Google Play release, and improvement, paying attention to usability, performance and behaviour in production.
  • Build effectively with agents: Use coding agents as part of your day-to-day: define clear tasks, provide context, evaluate generated code and make changes yourself when needed. Turn recurring problems into better agent instructions, tests and automated safeguards, and share what works with the team.
  • Build for trust: Make privacy, security, reliability and maintainability part of your decisions. Verify important failure cases, communicate what remains uncertain and make sound trade-offs about release readiness.

Our mobile teams work with Swift and SwiftUI on iOS, Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on Android, and Rust for shared cross-platform code. You’ll bring hands-on depth in at least one native platform. Experience with the other platform, or with Rust, is a plus but not expected, and the mix of platform and cross-platform work varies by team.

What you will bring:

  • Experience delivering production software: You can explain the problems you solved, the decisions you made and what you learned from maintaining your work after release.
  • Native mobile expertise: Strong, hands-on experience building and shipping production apps on iOS (Swift/SwiftUI) or Android (Kotlin/Jetpack Compose). You don’t need equal depth on both platforms, but you understand how your work interacts with the wider system and can contribute beyond your core platform.
  • Practical capability with coding agents: You’ve used agents to deliver working software and can explain how you guide them, improve unsuccessful approaches and verify their output.
  • Code fluency and engineering judgement: You can navigate unfamiliar code, trace behaviour, diagnose problems and make precise changes. You can assess an implementation regardless of who — or what — wrote it.
  • Product judgement: You connect technical choices to user needs, care about the quality of the experience and distinguish a useful reduction in scope from a shortcut that puts users at risk.
  • Ownership and collaboration: You move work forward through uncertainty, explain trade-offs clearly, seek out expertise and take responsibility for the outcome.
  • Technical leadership: You’ve led complex projects through ambiguous requirements, made decisions with lasting consequences and helped other engineers develop their judgement while remaining hands-on.

• What we offer:

  • Work that Matters: Millions of people trust Proton with their privacy. We answer only to our users — not advertisers, not investors with conflicting agendas, not governments.
  • A Great Team: Diverse, collaborative, and tight-knit, with people from MIT, Harvard, Stanford, Caltech, Cambridge, ETH, and more.
  • Technology: The right hardware and software to do your best work.
  • Learning & Development: We invest in your growth. Proton is one of the fastest ways to accelerate your career — real challenges, real ownership, from day one.
  • Employee Benefits: Strong health coverage, solid retirement options, generous leave, and wellness support.
  • Stock Options: From day one, you have a real stake in what we're building. When Proton wins, you win.
  • In-Person Collaboration: Offices across Geneva, Zürich, Barcelona, London, and more. You'll spend most of your time collaborating face-to-face.
  • Food: Lunch and snacks on us every day in the office.
  • Transport: We cover public transport, bike allowances, or parking — whichever works for you.
  • Flexible Working: You own your schedule. Outcomes matter more than clock-in times.
