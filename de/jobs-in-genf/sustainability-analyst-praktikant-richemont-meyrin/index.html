CONTEXT

The Sustainability Analyst Intern is responsible for supporting the Non-Financial Reporting (NFR) across Europe. This role supports European markets in maintaining robust, audit-ready reporting, enhancing data quality while working to harmonize reporting methodologies. Essential to the role's success is close collaboration with the Corporate Sustainability team and cross-functional stakeholders across the European region. This internship role is expected to start in February/March for a period of 6 months.

SCOPE: Richemont Europe, including RLGE Swiss Branch Legal Entity

YOUR MISSION

  • Provide assistance on Non-Financial Reporting (NFR) process to the European region and its market;
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure timely, accurate and audit-ready reporting;
  • Contribute to NFR methodology improvement and harmonization, supporting markets in enhancing data quality;
  • Collaborate with the Corporate Sustainability team, and share best practices across regions;
  • Support on local sustainability initiatives.

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

  • Proven Expertise: Previous first experience in sustainability preferred
  • Background: Bachelor’s degree in Sustainability, Economics or related field (a Master’s degree is a plus, but not required)
  • Business Acumen: Excellent written and verbal communication, detailed oriented and analytical, excellent command of Excel, PowerPoint, Word
  • Project & Stakeholder Management: Proven ability to meet deadlines and work on multiple projects simultaneously
  • Mindset: Self-motivated, strong work ethic, very strong organizational skills, with a strong emphasis on data quality and clear communication
  • Language: Fluent in English, good knowledge of French preferred
