Vollständige Beschreibung

Location: Zurich, Geneva Start Date: Ongoing Work Percentage: 80-100% About the Role Drive secure AI and cloud transformation for our clients We support our clients across their AI and cloud transformation with a Security by Design approach. You will design secure AI architectures that meet their business and security objectives and turn those designs into something they can actually adopt. Finding the talent that makes Deloitte unique Work with clients to understand their AI risks across cloud infrastructures, generative AI applications and agentic systems, as well as the regulations that apply to them. Run structured risk assessments to identify and prioritise Cloud and AI risks; and lead teams from high-level design through to implementation of the solutions you defined. How you can make an impact Help clients adopt AI and cloud security capabilities such as:

  • Secure cloud foundations — landing zones, identity and access management, network segmentation, encryption and key management.
  • Cloud posture management and workload protection — CSPM/CNAPP, infrastructure as code scanning, detection and response.
  • Secure generative AI applications — protection against prompt injection, input and output validation, secure RAG and integration patterns, aligned with the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications.
  • Secure agentic platforms — tool access control, decision logging, memory integrity, secure communication between agents, observability, aligned with the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications.
  • AI security posture management — continuous monitoring, vulnerability assessment, compliance tracking.
  • Secure AI services on AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
  • Secure model supply chain and deployment.
  • AI governance and compliance — EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, responsible for AI, maturity models. What you bring to succeed
  • 2–3 years (Consultant) or 5+ years (Senior Consultant) in a technical advisory role in cybersecurity, cloud security or AI security — consulting or in-house.
  • A degree in IT, cybersecurity, computer science, machine learning, or a related field.
  • A solid grounding in cloud security and secure development (DevSecOps, CI/CD pipelines, supply chain), plus working knowledge of several of the following: architectures for agentic systems, threat modelling for AI systems, securing cloud infrastructure for AI workloads, AI security frameworks, the AI and ML services of AWS, Azure or GCP.
  • Hands-on exposure to security tooling — CSPM, vulnerability scanning, threat modelling — and ideally AI-SPM or model scanning tools.
  • Fluent English is required. German and/or French are an advantage. What sets you apart
  • Certifications such as CISSP, CISM or CCSP; AWS Certified Security – Specialty or Azure Security Engineer; or AI-focused certifications (CAISP, CompTIA SecAI+, SANS/GIAC GAIPS or GASAE).
  • Python or a similar language.
  • We don't expect anyone to cover all of this. Tell us where your strengths are. Your team You will join a diverse, international, and highly collaborative team of cyber security professionals who are passionate about helping organisations strengthen their resilience against cyber threats. Most importantly, we foster an open and inclusive culture where people are encouraged to bring new ideas, challenge the status quo, and shape the future of cyber defence. What makes our team different is the opportunity to work across the full cyber defence lifecycle. One day you may be helping a client define their future Cyber Defence Centre strategy, the next designing security monitoring capabilities, improving incident response processes, or supporting the implementation of leading security technologies. We believe that great careers are built through challenging work, continuous learning, and strong support. You will benefit from hands-on experience, dedicated mentoring, professional certifications, and collaboration with experts across Deloitte's global network.
