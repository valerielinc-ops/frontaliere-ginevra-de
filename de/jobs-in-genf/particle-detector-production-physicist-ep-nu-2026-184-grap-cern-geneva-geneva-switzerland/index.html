Vollständige Beschreibung

Join the CERN Neutrino Platform and contribute to the DUNE (Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment), an international scientific collaboration at the forefront of neutrino physics research. We are looking for a Detector Physicist with a background in High Energy Physics and specialised expertise in Liquid Argon (LAr) Time Projection Chamber (TPC) detectors. The successful candidate will take responsibility for specific technical activities related to the DUNE Vertical Drift Far Detector, focusing on the assembly, testing, characterisation and validation of detector components and systems, in close collaboration with scientists and engineers at the CERN Neutrino Platform.

Your responsibilities

  • Carry out the testing and characterisation of Charge Readout Planes for the DUNE Vertical Drift Far Detector.
  • Perform the assembly and validation of Charge Readout Units and their associated readout electronics under cryogenic conditions.
  • Coordinate and follow up on technical activities with industrial partners involved in the production and assembly of LAr TPC electrodes.
  • Analyse and assess the quality of manufactured detector components against the required specifications.
  • Plan and carry out the testing and validation of detector systems, analyse their performance and document the results.
  • This role includes team supervision responsibilities.

Your profile

  • Manufacturing and assembly of large printed circuit boards (PCBs) for the readout electrodes of particle detectors.
  • Quality Assurance and Quality Control (QA/QC) of detector components.
  • Testing and characterisation of small- and large-scale particle detectors.
  • Detector readout and Data Acquisition (DAQ) systems.

Skills

  • Knowledge of signal formation and process in noble liquid-based detector.
  • Proficiency in software development in C++ and Python for detector data analysis.
  • Expertise in developing program of data acquisition systems to control and monitor real-time detector performance.
  • Excellent communication skills, flexibility and adaptability, with the ability to work effectively in large international collaborations.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • You have a professional background in Applied Physics, Detector Technologies (or a related field) and have either:
  • a Master's degree with 2 to 6 years of post-graduation professional experience;
  • or a PhD with no more than 3 years of post-graduation professional experience.
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.
