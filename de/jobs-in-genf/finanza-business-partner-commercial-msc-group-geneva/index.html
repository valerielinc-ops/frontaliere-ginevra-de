Where passion meets opportunity

The best of your adventures is the one you have yet to sail!

Your Purpose

We are looking for a structured, analytical, and proactive Finance Business Partner to lead financial planning, cost control, and performance analysis for Cost of Sales, while providing finance business partnering across Markets. The role will focus on understanding cost drivers, assessing commercial efficiency, and supporting decisions that improve profitability and resource allocation. Working across Corporate functions and Markets, the position will ensure that expenditure, contracts, and investments are aligned with approved priorities, commercial plans, and expected business benefits.

Your Impact

Budgeting, Forecasting, Actuals & Cost of Sales Planning

  • Manage budgeting, forecasting, reporting, and month-end close for Cost of Sales and commercial expenditure across Corporate and Markets.
  • Analyze cost drivers, financial variances, risks, and opportunities, providing recommendations to improve profitability.
  • Partner with Corporate and Market directors to align financial planning with booking volumes, revenue, distribution mix, and commercial objectives.
  • Monitor costs, revenue, and contribution margins across Markets and distribution channels to support resource allocation and financial efficiency.

Business Partnership, Cost Control & Contract Support

  • Act as a finance business partner across Corporate functions and Markets, ensuring financial governance and cost control.
  • Review commercial agreements, supplier proposals, and contractual commitments, assessing financial risks and cost optimization opportunities.
  • Collaborate with Procurement, Legal, and Commercial teams to improve cost efficiency, financial controls, and accountability.
  • Monitor purchase orders, accruals, and spending trends, recommending corrective actions when necessary.

Investment Appraisal & Performance Tracking

  • Evaluate commercial initiatives, investments, and out-of-budget requests to ensure alignment with business priorities.
  • Develop and assess business cases, including profitability, ROI, payback, cost savings, and financial risks.
  • Support investment prioritization and resource allocation through financial analysis and recommendations.
  • Track financial performance of approved initiatives and commercial, IT, and Digital projects against budgets and expected benefits.

Performance Analysis & Management Reporting

  • Deliver management reporting on Cost of Sales, commercial expenditure, and profitability against budgets and forecasts.
  • Analyze distribution costs, commissions, incentives, cost per booking, and contribution margins across Markets and channels.
  • Identify financial risks, cost pressures, and opportunities to improve commercial performance and efficiency.
  • Perform scenario, sensitivity, and post-investment analysis to support strategic decisions and cost optimization.

Your Journey so far

  • Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, or a related field; a Master’s degree or MBA is a plus.
  • 10+ years of experience in FP&A, controlling, or finance business partnering, preferably with exposure to Cost of Sales, distribution economics, and international Commercial or Market organizations.
  • Strong experience in budgeting, forecasting, month-end close, variance analysis, and management reporting within an international environment.
  • Strong understanding of Cost of Sales drivers and commercial economics, including commissions, distribution costs, incentives, cost per booking, cost-to-revenue ratios, channel mix, and contribution margin.
  • Experience in business cases, profitability analysis, investment tracking, commercial contracts, purchase orders, accruals, and supplier commitments.
  • High proficiency in Excel, Power BI or other reporting tools, ERP systems such as SAP, and CPM systems such as Tagetik.
  • Ability to work effectively with AI-enabled tools to support financial analysis, forecasting, reporting, and process automation; critically validate outputs and apply sound judgment, data confidentiality, and company governance requirements.

Your Essentials

  • Ability to partner with senior stakeholders, provide constructive challenge, and translate analysis into practical recommendations.
  • Ability to work cross-division and coordinate Corporate functions and Markets without direct hierarchical authority.
  • Strong analytical and communication skills, with the ability to critically assess and challenge existing processes, support their improvement, and proactively propose innovative solutions that enhance efficiency, controls, and decision-making.
  • Fluency in English; additional languages are an advantage.
