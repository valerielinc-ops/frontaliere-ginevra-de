Vollständige Beschreibung

The opportunity As an Assembler, you are responsible for the professional assembly of semi-finished and finished transformer products. Using hand and power tools as well as technical documentation, you carry out assigned assembly tasks accurately and reliably. You ensure that our products meet high quality and safety standards. You work closely with your team, document any deviations and, once sufficiently experienced, support the training of new employees.

How you'll make an impact

  • Assemble semi-finished and finished transformer products using hand and power tools
  • Read and interpret technical drawings, blueprints and specifications to ensure accurate assembly
  • Perform regular quality inspections on semi-finished and finished products, identifying and reporting defects or deviations
  • Document and report quality issues and inform supervisors about work progress
  • Consistently follow health and safety regulations and report any safety concerns or non-compliance
  • Maintain a clean, organized and well-structured workplace
  • Collaborate closely with team members and support the training of new employees in assembly processes and best practices once the required skills have been acquired
  • Participate in regular workplace safety meetings and training sessions, while following established work procedures and production schedules

Your background

  • Completed compulsory education or equivalent; technical education or certification is an advantage
  • Previous experience in assembly or a comparable role in a manufacturing or production environment
  • Basic understanding of electrical components and assembly processes
  • Ability to read and understand technical drawings, blueprints and documentation
  • Strong attention to detail, a quality-focused mindset and a high level of safety awareness
  • Good manual dexterity, physical ability to lift and handle heavy components, as well as a reliable and collaborative approach to work

More about us Hitachi Energy is shaping the future of energy with innovative technologies for a sustainable and reliable power supply. We offer a modern working environment, a strong team and the opportunity to contribute to forward-looking solutions.
