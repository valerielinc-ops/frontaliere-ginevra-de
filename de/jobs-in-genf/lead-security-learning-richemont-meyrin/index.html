Vollständige Beschreibung

Reference code: JR135822

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

YOUR MISSION

The Lead, Security Learning is responsible for defining and delivering the Group Security learning and capability-building strategy across Richemont. The role translates security priorities, evolving risks and stakeholder needs into scalable, engaging and measurable learning experiences that strengthen security culture, behaviours and operational capability across Group Functions, Regions and Maisons.

Working within Group Security Technology and in close partnership with Security subject-matter experts, Learning & Development, Human Resources, Communications and external providers, the role owns and coordinates the end-to-end learning lifecycle: needs analysis, learning architecture, content development, deployment, evaluation and continuous improvement.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

  • Security learning strategy and roadmap: Develop and maintain a multi-year security learning strategy and prioritised roadmap aligned with Group Security objectives, business risks, target audiences and Richemont learning standards.
  • Capability development and learning architecture: Assess capability needs and design coherent learning pathways, curricula and development programmes that strengthen security capabilities across target audiences.
  • Content development and delivery: Lead the design, development, curation and delivery of high-quality blended learning, including e-learning, instructor-led sessions, virtual workshops, simulations, videos, podcasts, job aids, assessments and learning communities.
  • Learning platform and operations: Coordinate publication, enrolment, assignment, learner support, completion tracking and content lifecycle management through Richemont learning platforms and collaboration tools; ensure accurate learning records and version control.
  • Stakeholder and SME management: Partner with Group Security experts, Maisons, regions and functions to gather requirements, validate technical accuracy, localise content and embed learning into relevant business processes.
  • Change, communication and adoption: Develop launch and communication plans with relevant partners, build stakeholder sponsorship and drive adoption of priority learning programmes across a global and culturally diverse organisation.
  • Measurement and reporting: Define KPIs and evaluation methods covering participation, completion, knowledge acquisition, learner experience, behavioural application and business impact.
  • Vendor, budget and resource management: Manage external learning partners, statements of work, translations, licences and allocated budgets, ensuring efficient and effective utilisation of tools, technologies, and personnel.
  • Continuous improvement and innovation: Use feedback, analytics, lessons learned and emerging learning practices to improve the portfolio; monitor relevant technologies, including responsible use of AI-enabled learning solutions.
  • Team and community enablement: Lead or mentor internal and external learning professionals and contributors; establish reusable standards, templates and guidance that enable SMEs to create effective learning content.

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent professional experience in Learning & Development, Education, Organisational Development, Communications, Security or a related field.
  • Proven experience designing and delivering enterprise learning strategies and programmes in a global organisation.
  • Experience in security, risk, compliance, technology or another technical learning environment is strongly preferred.
  • Relevant learning, instructional design or project management certification is an advantage.
  • Strong command of adult-learning principles, instructional design, curriculum development and blended learning.
  • Hands-on experience with learning management systems, content-authoring tools and digital collaboration platforms.
  • Working knowledge of Articulate 360, Microsoft 365 and relevant media or design tools
  • Adobe Creative Suite experience is an advantage.
  • Excellent stakeholder management skills.
  • Clear and engaging written, verbal, facilitation and presentation skills.
  • Strong analytical judgement, attention to detail and focus on outcomes.
  • Comfort working autonomously across cultures, functions and seniority levels.
  • Collaborative, pragmatic, curious and committed to continuous learning.
  • Fluency in English is required
  • French and additional languages are strong assets.
  • High standards of integrity and appropriate handling of security-sensitive information.
